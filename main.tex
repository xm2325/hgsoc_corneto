\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Question.} We ask whether high-grade serous ovarian cancer (HGSOC)
ovarian cancer models (OCMs) contain metabolic--regulatory states that are
reproducible across public cohorts, robust to repeated patients and modelling
choices, enriched relative to labelled reference samples, and supported by
independent mechanisms.  This is a conjunction of testable claims, not one
binary assertion.

\textbf{Evidence.} A deterministic metadata contract links \ManifestRunCount{}
RNA-seq runs (\FastqObjectCount{} FASTQ objects; exactly \FastqByteCount{}
compressed bytes) to \PrimaryOCMCount{} primary HGSOC tumour OCMs from
\PrimaryPatientCount{} patients.  Fixed-rank pooled and cohort-local NMF fits
show substantial but incomplete \emph{same-data} concordance: rank-3 adjusted
Rand indices span 0.377--0.723 and mapped agreement spans 0.727--0.889.  Rank 2
has stronger pooled stability than rank 3, falsifying any claim that rank 3 is
the uniquely supported dimension.  One lexicographic one-OCM-per-patient
sensitivity yields ARI 0.842 and agreement 0.942, but does not establish
robustness over all valid patient representatives.  A receipt-valid
multi-condition regulatory grid contains 45 fits over nine nominal
regularisation values.  At nominal $\lambda=0.001$, patient de-duplication
retains high edge overlap (union Jaccard 0.890), whereas a bundled richer graph
policy changes inputs, outputs, and search depth and produces low pooled
agreement (union Jaccard 0.202).  This demonstrates material graph-policy
sensitivity, not a stable causal core.  Near-optimal sampling in E-MTAB-14568
produces 26 non-empty receipt-valid ensembles and one blocked zero-edge
optimum.

\textbf{Boundary.} Full-cohort metabolic solutions, tumour--reference
enrichment, OCM-specific TPI1 dependency, and treatment-response claims are not
declared positive here.  Exact OCM-linked paclitaxel AUC, raw GI50, and
cumulative exposure are absent from the validated public table.  We therefore
report a layered evidence atlas: what is supported, what has been falsified,
what remains weakened or pending, and the observations that would overturn
each statement.
\end{abstract}

\section{Answer first: the central proposition is only partly supported}

The project centres on the following proposition:

\begin{quote}
HGSOC OCMs contain metabolic--regulatory states that reproduce across cohorts,
remain robust to patient repetition and model choice, are enriched relative to
stroma or other labelled references, and receive independent mechanistic
support.
\end{quote}

No single analysis can establish that conjunction.  We therefore split it into
13 claims (Appendix~\ref{app:claims}).  The frozen sample universe is
\supported.  NMF pooled-versus-local concordance is measurable, but independent
cross-cohort reproduction is \weakened{} because each tested cohort also
contributes to the pooled fit.  Rank 3 as the unique latent dimension is
\falsified.  Regulatory patient de-duplication is encouraging under one
deterministic selection, while graph-policy invariance is \weakened.  A
cross-cohort metabolic state, tumour--reference enrichment, and independent
mechanistic replication remain \pending.  OCM-specific TPI1 dependency,
paclitaxel response, and intrinsic/acquired-resistance estimands are \blocked{}
by absent validated inputs or results.

These labels have operational meanings.  ``Supported under tested conditions''
requires a source or receipt and survives the perturbations already performed;
it is not proof.  ``Weakened'' means a narrower wording survives but a stronger
one does not.  ``Falsified'' identifies a claim contradicted by an observed
counter-result.  ``Blocked'' means the estimand is not identifiable from the
current inputs.  ``Pending'' means the inputs may exist but the specified test
has not yet supplied a valid answer.

At rank 3, ARI ranges from 0.377 to 0.723 and mapped assignment agreement from
0.727 to 0.889 (Table~\ref{tab:nmf}).  The pooled state--study contingency test
has $\chi^2=8.455$, $p=0.207$, and Cramer's $V=0.265$.  Failure to reject the
null does not prove absence of a study effect; the non-zero effect size and
small cohorts instead motivate a leave-one-study-out projection and a
patient-blocked permutation null.

One deterministic 52-OCM sensitivity selects a single row per patient by
lexicographic study/run order.  Its ARI is 0.842, NMI 0.825, and mapped
agreement 0.942 relative to the corresponding pooled assignments.  Because
this is only one of $2^6\times3=192$ valid one-per-patient choices for the six
pairs and one triplet, it supports only a narrow outcome-blind sensitivity.
It does not establish general robustness to patient representation.

\subsection{Regulatory inference is stable to one patient selection but not to graph policy}

CollecTRI signed TF--target interactions\cite{collectri} and OmniPath directed
signed interactions\cite{omnipath} provide prior evidence for CORNETO
CARNIVALFlow\cite{liu2019,corneto}.  The true multi-condition grid uses
\[
\lambda_{\mathrm{reported}}\in
\{0,0.001,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.25,0.5,1,2\}.
\]
Under the recorded CORNETO convention that condition errors are summed, the
implementation sets
$\lambda_{\mathrm{solver}}=n\lambda_{\mathrm{reported}}$ for $n$ included
conditions.  Small exact-equivalence tests can falsify that convention if the
objective implementation changes.

The scientific summary validates five modes (pooled plus four cohort-local
fits) at nine values: 45 receipt-valid optimisations.  At nominal
$\lambda=0.001$, a deterministic 52-patient regulatory fit has union Jaccard
0.890 and mean per-sample Jaccard 0.899 against the 52 shared rows of the
60-OCM fit.  This again evaluates one patient selection, not all 192.

A second sensitivity changes the bundled graph policy from at most 3 inputs,
6 outputs, and depth 3 to at most 5 inputs, 10 outputs, and depth 4.  At the
same nominal $\lambda=0.001$, pooled union Jaccard falls to 0.202 and mean
per-sample Jaccard to 0.108; cohort union Jaccards range 0.108--0.143.  Because
three design dimensions change together, the result establishes material
\emph{graph-policy sensitivity}.  It cannot identify PKN breadth as the sole
cause, and low edge overlap does not logically prove that an untested stable
module or core exists.

\subsection{Near-optimal regulatory solutions expose uncertainty hidden by one incumbent}

For 27 E-MTAB-14568 primary OCMs at $\lambda=0.1$, the final alternative-optima
summary contains 26 non-empty receipt-valid ensembles and one
\texttt{blocked\_no\_selected\_edges} condition.  Each non-empty sample accepted
30 alternatives; medians are 5.5 incumbent edges, 6 union edges, and 5 core
edges.  The median pairwise Jaccard is 1.0, but individual-sample receipt values
include substantially lower agreement.  The median therefore cannot be used to
claim invariance for every OCM.  A mathematically optimal zero-edge solution is
blocked, not re-labelled as biological inactivity.

The Meeson toy benchmarks provide a separate counterexample.  Two candidate
orders retain opposite interchangeable routes (retained-set Jaccard 0).  A
global formulation removes that iteration order but has two symmetric optima
(growth 6.000001; objective $-1$).  A two-condition joint toy reduces the active
reaction union from four to three by sharing one route.  These are mechanism
tests of the code; the smaller union is mechanically encouraged by the joint
objective and is not biological superiority.

\subsection{Metabolic, reference-enrichment, TPI1 and phenotype conclusions remain gated}

The pinned Human-GEM v1.4.1 fingerprint has SHA256
\texttt{57d1b137f0c90d83a3e4f9a8225d74d37523594e6ee99f622b160a014d9f7050},
13,096 reactions, 8,400 metabolites, 3,628 genes, a complete GPR partition, and
SBML biomass ID \texttt{R\_biomass\_human}\cite{human1}.  TPI1 maps to
\texttt{ENSG00000111669} and reaction \texttt{HMR\_4391}.  These are model
identity checks only.  They do not show that TPI1 deletion affects any OCM.

The pooled-60 metabolic input gate is valid (60 samples, 52 patients, 60,609
genes), but an input gate is not a solution.  Full-cohort and pooled metabolic
claims require receipt-valid objectives, growth targets, flux summaries,
alternative-optimum/FVA sensitivity, and comparison against a frozen null.
Accordingly, this snapshot does not claim a cross-cohort metabolic state.

The manifest retains 33 stroma runs, two cell-line controls, 17 non-HGSOC or
ambiguous tumour runs, four tumour runs not matched to Tighe Table S1, and one
non-representative duplicate.  These categories can answer useful secondary
questions, but only with study-aware estimands.  Tumour--stroma inference should
be paired within accession where possible; cell lines are descriptive controls,
and non-HGSOC samples are heterogeneous references rather than a single
comparator.  No such contrast is declared complete here.

Finally, the frozen manifest contains zero exact OCM-linked paclitaxel AUC,
raw GI50, and cumulative exposure rows.  Chemotherapy-naive-at-biopsy is not a
substitute for zero cumulative exposure.  Drug-response prediction,
intrinsic-resistance, acquired-resistance, and treatment-caused rewiring are
therefore not identifiable from the validated public table.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{What the current evidence answers}

The strongest result is methodological rather than translational.  The project
establishes a traceable 60-OCM/52-patient contract, demonstrates incomplete
same-data NMF concordance, falsifies rank 3 as the uniquely stable rank, and
shows that regulatory conclusions depend materially on graph policy and
optimum choice.  These observations justify reporting distributions,
recurrence, modules defined independently of the tested solution, and explicit
claim limits rather than a single attractive network.

Patient repetition matters conceptually even though one de-duplicated analysis
has high agreement.  Seven of 52 patients contribute repeated primary models;
their 15 rows are one quarter of the 60-OCM matrix.  A defensible robustness
claim requires all 192 valid one-per-patient selections or inverse-multiplicity
weights, compared with a study-matched random-deletion null.  Patient-grouped
cross-validation solves leakage, not weighting.

\subsection{What would change the conclusion}

Independent cross-cohort NMF reproduction requires fitting without the tested
study and projecting its held-out samples.  A regulatory stable-core claim
requires a core definition frozen before testing, recurrence above a
degree-aware graph null, and stability across lambda, graph-policy factors,
patient selections, and alternative optima.  A metabolic-state claim requires
valid cohort and pooled receipts plus robustness to medium, expression
transform, growth target, candidate budget and feasible-flux ranges.  A TPI1
dependency claim requires OCM-specific constrained WT/knockout effects and an
independent perturbation test.  A response claim requires exact outcomes and
patient-grouped nested validation against simpler baselines.

\subsection{Claim hierarchy for the eventual paper}

The eventual confirmatory narrative should proceed in this order:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item define the frozen OCM/patient universe and reference categories;
  \item demonstrate held-out, patient-aware expression-state transportability;
  \item quantify regulatory and metabolic recurrence under pre-specified nulls;
  \item test patient, graph, solver and optimum sensitivities;
  \item test tumour--reference enrichment within study;
  \item freeze targets/modules before external perturbation validation; and
  \item only then introduce exact drug response and exposure.
\end{enumerate}

This ordering prevents cohort labels, response values, or post-hoc literature
matches from selecting the network that is later described as validated.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Evidence provenance and deterministic manuscript build}

Every manuscript statement is classified as an observed source fact,
deterministic derivation, model output, or configured/planned analysis.  Local
counts are rebuilt by \path{scripts/build_manuscript_evidence.py}.  Remote model
values are admitted only through a receipt snapshot containing the original
Roihu path, SHA256, selected JSON/TSV fields, retrieval date, and claim limit.
The original receipt remains authoritative.  Slurm status, log existence, and
status-register prose do not substitute for a scientific receipt.

The generator validates the 117 unique runs, the mutually exclusive disposition
counts, 60 primary OCMs, 52 patients, 9/13/11/27 study split, 234 FASTQ objects,
and exactly 477,762,645,114 compressed bytes.  It generates Table~\ref{tab:cohorts},
Table~\ref{tab:nmf}, the complete study registry, and the claim/failure tables.
The source and generated files are committed so the arXiv build does not depend
on running project-specific Python.

\subsection{How Table 1 is derived from BioStudies and ENA}

For each E-MTAB accession, the BioStudies/ArrayExpress IDF identifies the study
and publication, while the SDRF supplies one row per assay relationship with
source labels, tumour/stroma/control annotations, passage or fraction labels,
and ENA links.  The ENA project \texttt{read\_run.tsv} report supplies stable
run, experiment and sample accessions plus the two FASTQ URLs, MD5 values and
byte counts.  Two paired FASTQ objects map to each of the 117 unique runs.

Source labels are parsed into canonical OCM and material class by the committed
metadata code, then cross-referenced to Tighe Table S1.  A primary row satisfies
all four predicates: tumour material, exact Tighe-screen match, strict HGSOC
histotype, and representative RNA library.  The current only later-passage
duplicate exclusion is OCM46-3 P14, for which P4 is retained.  Missing or
ambiguous histotype remains ambiguous; suffixes are never guessed.  The
mutually exclusive exclusion order is primary, stroma, control, unmatched
Tighe tumour, non-HGSOC/ambiguous tumour, and non-representative duplicate.

\subsection{NMF estimand and falsification}

NMF is an unsupervised technical decomposition of log-transformed expression,
not a measured pathway activity or clinical subtype.  Pooled and cohort-local
rank-3 labels are matched by maximum overlap before ARI, NMI and agreement are
computed; loading Spearman correlation uses common loading genes.  The current
comparison is not independent because the pooled fit contains all cohorts.
The required falsification test for a transportability claim is
leave-one-study-out fitting followed by held-out projection and comparison with
a patient-blocked permutation null.  Candidate rank, feature selection and
bootstrap stability must be reported rather than selected by the desired
biological narrative.

\subsection{Regulatory estimand and sensitivity}

CARNIVALFlow selects a signed subgraph connecting expression-derived TF
evidence to a versioned OmniPath search graph.  TF scores are inferred evidence,
not protein measurements.  Pooled and cohort fits use a frozen input bundle,
record condition sets and source hashes, and solve all conditions in one
multi-condition object.  The lambda grid is outcome-blind.  The patient and
richer-policy comparisons reported here are fixed at nominal $\lambda=0.001$;
this is an illustrative low-penalty sensitivity, not a phenotype-selected
primary lambda.

The richer-policy comparison is factorially confounded because it changes input
cap, output cap and path depth together.  A future decomposition must vary one
factor at a time and compare against degree-preserving PKN and sign-permutation
nulls.  Edges should be summarised by ensemble frequency and predefined modules,
not declared causal because one optimum contains them.

\subsection{Metabolic and Meeson boundary}

The Meeson sequential algorithm is audited separately from new global and
joint formulations.  Exact recreation of the public 49-OCM flux table is
blocked because the repository points to an unavailable author-local
expression-plus-growth input.  The output labels are therefore preserved
verbatim in Appendix~\ref{app:paperocm}; uncertain aliases are not forced into
the Tighe canonical namespace.  Human-GEM identity, toy mechanisms, and public
outputs can be audited without claiming exact numerical reproduction.

\subsection{Reference and phenotype analysis plan}

Tumour--stroma contrasts will use within-study, preferably within-patient,
differences and study-specific effects before any meta-analysis.  Cell-line
controls are QC references only.  HGSOC versus non-HGSOC analysis must preserve
reported histotype because the reference category includes ambiguous ovarian
cancer labels.  Set subtraction alone is insufficient; effect size,
uncertainty, multiplicity control and model sensitivity are required.

The phenotype contract specifies paclitaxel AUC as primary and positive raw
GI50 in nM, analysed on a $\log_{10}$ scale, as secondary.  Exact cumulative
exposure and verified sample order are required for intrinsic/acquired
estimands.  No value is digitised from a plot or replaced by a proxy.

\section{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Four public RNA accessions are not four independent biological
  replications: patient OCM74 crosses studies, and pooled NMF contains each
  cohort used in the local comparison.
  \item One lexicographic patient-balanced set is not a sampling distribution.
  \item Regulatory graph-policy sensitivity is bundled across three factors;
  no single-factor PKN conclusion is available.
  \item Expression-derived TF activity, selected signalling edges, and sparse
  metabolic fluxes are model-supported hypotheses, not direct measurements.
  \item The current public phenotype boundary prevents response and treatment-
  associated claims.
  \item Some paper panels report exact counts but not machine-readable complete
  OCM ID sets.  Unknown members remain unknown.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Taylor-group publications, datasets and reusable resources}
\label{app:resources}

This atlas uses a project-relevance rule rather than claiming to reproduce an
entire career bibliography: an item is included when it generated an OCM,
experimentally interrogated an OCM mechanism, supplied one of the analysed
datasets, or directly defined the metabolic/regulatory method.  The University
of Manchester profile is the moving catalogue for outputs outside that scope
\cite{taylorprofile}.  Counts on that profile are dynamic and are therefore not
used as scientific results.

\subsection{Reading map}

Nelson 2020 establishes the living OCM biobank and naming system
\cite{nelson2020}; Barnes 2021 supplies histotype-aware transcriptional
classification context\cite{barnes2021}; Coulson-Gilmer 2021 and Golder 2022
develop replication-stress and drug-combination mechanisms
\cite{coulsongilmer2021,golder2022}; Littler 2025 and Tighe 2025 address
SUMOylation, taxol/MDR1 and cabazitaxel\cite{littler2025,tighe2025}; Meeson 2024

Topham 2015, Williams 2020 and Bronder 2021 provide MYC/mitotic-fate,

\cite{topham2015,williams2020,bronder2021}.  Coulson-Gilmer 2024 directly tests
nucleoside rescue in OCM109 and OCM246\cite{coulsongilmer2024}.

\subsection{Doctoral theses as methods companions}

Five theses add detail that is not always recoverable from article methods:
Golder documents the OCM drug-profiling platform\cite{golderthesis2021}; Bronder
separates engineered FNE chromosomal-instability systems from OCM evidence
\cite{bronderthesis2021}; Morgan develops PAR/PARG therapeutic work
\cite{morganthesis2021}; Owen develops the MYC--SUMOylation programme
\cite{owenthesis2023}; and Meeson records the OCM metabolic integration and its

resource, not an independent replication of a result that first appeared in

\section{Evidence and access boundaries}

The four RNA studies and their metadata are public.  Exact OCM-linked
paclitaxel AUC, raw GI50 and cumulative exposure are not present in the
validated public table.  Tighe 2025 Table S1 and S2 are locally frozen, but
some paper panels report only counts or non-machine-readable example IDs.
The Meeson 49-OCM output is public while its generating input is absent.
E-MTAB-16770 addresses cell-line perturbation, not the observational OCM
question.  Living cultures, some imaging and additional clinical annotation
may require author contact or controlled access.  Unknown information is not
reconstructed from figures, suffixes or convenient proxies.
